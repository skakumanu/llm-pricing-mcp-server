# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-31

This policy applies to paid plans bought through Stripe. This document is a template awaiting legal review and is not in force until the notice at the top of the page is gone.

## Full refund within 14 days

You can get a full refund of your first payment if you ask within 14 days of your first payment.

## Pro-rated refund after 14 days

After 14 days a refund is pro-rated for the unused part of the current paid period, on request.

## The owner reviews every request

Refunds are never granted by software. The owner reviews every request, decides it and tells you the outcome. The support assistant cannot refund, credit, discount or change a plan.

## How to ask

Ask the support assistant on [the chat page](/chat?mode=support) for a refund. It opens an escalation in the owner's queue and gives you a reference. Keep the reference: you can ask the assistant for its status at any time. Do not paste card numbers or keys into chat.

## Disputes and double charges

Disputes, chargebacks and double charges are handled the same way: they are placed in the owner's queue and reviewed personally. If you dispute a payment with your bank, we may suspend the account while the dispute is open.

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time in the Stripe customer portal ("Manage Billing" on [the billing page](/billing)). Cancelling stops future charges and returns you to Free at the end of the paid period. Cancelling does not by itself refund a payment already made.
